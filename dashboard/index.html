    Quick answers
    
          
    Orders are not printing
    Check the status pill at the top says Connected, printing is not paused, and the right printer is chosen in Printer & settings. Then press Print test page. If the test works but orders don't, open Orders and use Retry.
    
          
    It prints a small block or garbage
    Turn on Safe print mode in Printer & settings → Print quality, save, and print a test page again.
    
          
    The page is cut or too small
    Set Page scaling to "Fit to page" and Paper size to your printer's paper (usually A4). ID and passport sheets always print at exact size.
    
          
    It says my key is used on another computer
    Your plan allows a limited number of computers (Starter 1, Pro 5). Sign out on the other PC, or contact support to reset it.
    
          
    Where do I see my earnings and change prices?
    Open Shop & earnings. Orders and reports are on the first tab, prices, your PIN and your plan are under Plan & settings.
    
          
    How does the free trial work?
    You approve UPI AutoPay or a card. Razorpay charges ₹5 to verify it and refunds it. Your plan is charged only after the 7-day trial. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.
